⚛ Physics Textbooks
The texts I reference most in tutoring sessions and research. From intro-level to graduate. Amazon affiliate links (tag: fissionlab-20).
Fundamentals of Physics Standard
Halliday, Resnick & Walker · 10th Edition
The gold standard intro physics textbook. Covers mechanics, electromagnetism, thermodynamics, optics, and modern physics with rigorous but accessible explanations. Used in most university physics sequences.
Dr. Preston's note Start here if you are taking introductory calculus-based physics. The worked examples are clear and the problem sets build exactly the problem-solving instincts you need.
Amazon affiliate link View on Amazon →
The Feynman Lectures on Physics Conceptual
Richard P. Feynman · 3 Volume Set
The most insightful physics writing in existence. Not a problem-solving textbook — a deep conceptual companion. Read alongside your primary text to build genuine understanding rather than formula recall.
Dr. Preston's note When a student tells me they "get the math but not the physics," this is what I assign. Feynman's explanation of electromagnetism alone is worth the price of the whole set.
Amazon affiliate link View on Amazon →
Physics for Scientists & Engineers Problem Sets
Serway & Jewett · 10th Edition
Strong problem-solving emphasis. Better problem variety than Halliday/Resnick for engineering students. The two-volume set covers mechanics, thermal physics, electromagnetism, and modern physics comprehensively.
Dr. Preston's note My go-to recommendation for students in engineering physics sequences. The end-of-chapter problems scale well from straightforward to genuinely challenging.
