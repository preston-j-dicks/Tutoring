Complete Breakdown

All 12 AFOQT Subtests

Timing, question count, composite weights, and the single most important tip for each subtest.

1. Verbal Analogies
25 Q · 8 min
Affects: Verbal · Academic Aptitude
Practice the form "A is to B as C is to ___." Focus on relationship types: part-to-whole, cause-effect, synonym, function.
2. Arithmetic Reasoning
25 Q · 29 min
Affects: Quantitative · Academic Aptitude
Word problems testing applied math. Sketch the scenario, identify what's being solved, and watch unit conversions carefully.
3. Word Knowledge
25 Q · 5 min
Affects: Verbal · Academic Aptitude
12 seconds per question. Build vocabulary with GRE/military word lists. Recognize Latin/Greek roots for unknown words.
4. Math Knowledge
25 Q · 22 min
Affects: Quantitative · Academic Aptitude · Pilot · CSO
Algebra, geometry, and basic trig. This subtest hits the Pilot composite — prioritize it. Know your exponent rules cold.
5. Reading Comprehension
25 Q · 38 min
Affects: Verbal · Academic Aptitude
Read the questions first, then skim the passage for answers. The correct answer is always directly supported by the text.
6. Situational Judgment
50 Q · 35 min
Affects: Officer Leadership Composite
Think like an AF officer: chain of command, mission first, people always. The "best" answer is usually the most professional and proactive one.
7. Self-Description Inventory
220 Q · 40 min
Affects: Officer Leadership Composite
Personality inventory — answer honestly and consistently. Inconsistent responses are algorithmically flagged. Be authentic.
8. Physical Science
20 Q · 10 min
Affects: Academic Aptitude · CSO
Physics, chemistry, basic biology. Know Newton's laws, Ohm's law, the periodic table basics, and speed of sound/light.
9. Table Reading
40 Q · 7 min
Affects: Pilot · CSO
10 seconds per question — pure speed and accuracy. Practice moving your eyes to the correct row/column intersection under time pressure.
10. Instrument Comprehension
25 Q · 5 min
Affects: Pilot · CSO
Read artificial horizon and compass simultaneously. Practice "level left bank right" orientation. This is trainable with repetition.
11. Block Counting
30 Q · 4.5 min
Affects: Pilot · CSO
Count how many blocks touch a numbered block. Rotate the image mentally. Work in a systematic pattern — top-to-bottom, front-to-back.
12. Aviation Information
20 Q · 8 min
Affects: Pilot · ABM
Study FAA Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge — it's free online. Focus on four forces of flight, flight controls, airport markings, and weather.

Common Questions

AFOQT FAQ

What is the AFOQT?
The Air Force Officer Qualifying Test is a standardized test used by the U.S. Air Force and Space Force to assess candidates for officer commissioning programs. It measures verbal, quantitative, and aviation-related aptitude.
How long does the AFOQT take?
Approximately 3.5 hours of testing time, not counting administrative time. The 12 subtests vary from 4.5 to 40 minutes each.
What is a competitive AFOQT score?
Minimums are Verbal 15 / Quantitative 10, but competitive candidates typically score 50+ on relevant composites. For pilot training, a Pilot composite of 25+ and PCSM of 50+ is generally competitive.
Can I retake the AFOQT?
You may take the AFOQT a maximum of two times total, with a mandatory 180-day wait between attempts. Your most recent score is the one that counts.
What subtests affect the Pilot composite?
Math Knowledge, Table Reading, Instrument Comprehension, and Aviation Information drive the Pilot composite. These four are where your prep time has the highest ROI if you're aiming for rated aviation.
How should I approach the Self-Description Inventory?
Answer honestly and consistently. It is a personality assessment and there are no trick answers. Inconsistent responses are algorithmically detected. Reflect your genuine leadership tendencies.
PHYSICS

Physics Study Resources

From classical mechanics through quantum theory — rigorous resources for serious students.

Classical Mechanics
Newton's laws, kinematics, work-energy theorem, momentum, and rotational dynamics. The foundation of all physics — master it before moving on.
Electromagnetism
Maxwell's equations, Gauss's law, Faraday induction, circuits, and electromagnetic waves. Griffiths is the gold standard reference.
Thermodynamics
Four laws of thermodynamics, entropy, Carnot efficiency, and heat engines. Understand entropy conceptually, not just mathematically.
Quantum Mechanics
Wave-particle duality, the Schrodinger equation, uncertainty principle, and quantum numbers. Visualization tools help enormously here.
Nuclear Physics
Radioactive decay, binding energy, fission, fusion, and cross sections. Directly relevant to AFOQT Physical Science and reactor engineering.
Modern Physics
Special relativity, time dilation, mass-energy equivalence, and particle physics. Krane's Modern Physics is the best single-volume survey.

AI / ML / DL

AI & Machine Learning Resources

From supervised learning fundamentals through deep neural networks and transformers.

ML Fundamentals
Supervised vs. unsupervised learning, bias-variance tradeoff, regularization, and model evaluation. Know cross-validation cold.
Neural Networks
Backpropagation, activation functions (ReLU, sigmoid, softmax), gradient descent variants, and weight initialization strategies.
Deep Learning
CNNs for vision, RNNs/LSTMs for sequences, and transformers for attention-based tasks. Goodfellow's textbook remains the definitive reference.
Python & NumPy
Vectorization, broadcasting, array operations, and efficient data pipelines. Writing non-loop NumPy code is a fundamental ML skill.
Probability & Stats for ML
Maximum likelihood estimation, Bayes theorem, common distributions, and hypothesis testing. Bishop's PRML makes the probabilistic framework explicit.
NLP & Computer Vision
Tokenization, word embeddings, attention mechanisms, object detection, and image segmentation. Transformers unify both domains now.

SAT / ACT

SAT & ACT Study Resources

Strategic prep for both exams — math, reading, writing, and the test-taking tactics that move the needle.

Heart of Algebra
Linear equations, systems of equations, and inequalities. This is the highest-frequency SAT math domain — drill it until it is automatic.
Passport to Advanced Math
Quadratics, polynomials, rational expressions, and function notation. Factoring and completing the square are non-negotiable skills.
Problem Solving & Data Analysis
Ratios, percentages, unit conversion, and interpreting graphs and tables. These questions reward careful reading over complex computation.
Geometry & Trig
Circle theorems, triangle similarity, SOHCAHTOA, and special right triangles. Know the unit circle for any ACT trig questions.
Reading Strategy
Evidence-based reading with vocabulary in context. Always return to the passage — every correct answer is directly supported by the text.
Writing & Language
Grammar rules, punctuation, and rhetorical effectiveness. Read answer choices aloud — the wrong answers often sound wrong when heard.

MATHEMATICS

Mathematics Study Resources

Calculus, linear algebra, differential equations, and probability — the mathematical toolkit for science and engineering.

Limits & Derivatives
Epsilon-delta definitions, chain rule, product rule, implicit differentiation, and L'Hopital's rule. Understand limits before memorizing derivative rules.
Integration
Fundamental Theorem of Calculus, u-substitution, integration by parts, partial fractions, and improper integrals. Tenenbaum has 500+ solved problems.
Multivariable Calculus
Partial derivatives, gradients, directional derivatives, double and triple integrals, and vector fields. Essential for physics and ML.
Linear Algebra
Matrix operations, determinants, eigenvalues, eigenvectors, and singular value decomposition. Axler's approach builds real conceptual depth.
Differential Equations
Separable equations, first-order linear, second-order constant-coefficient, Laplace transforms, and systems of ODEs. Tenenbaum covers them all.
Probability & Combinatorics
Counting principles, permutations, combinations, discrete and continuous distributions, expected value, and variance. Foundational for ML and statistics.
