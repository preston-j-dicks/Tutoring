Tutoring Philosophy

🎯
Diagnose First
Every student starts with a diagnostic. I don't waste your time reviewing what you already know — we attack your specific weak areas from day one.
⏱️
Speed Is a Skill
The AFOQT rewards trained speed as much as knowledge. We drill under real time pressure so test day feels routine, not panicked.
🧠
Understand, Don't Memorize
Memorizing answers won't work — the test varies. I teach the underlying concepts and reasoning so you can answer any variant of the question.
🤝
Honest Feedback
I tell you what the score means, what it will take to improve, and how realistic your timeline is. No empty encouragement — just actionable truth.
