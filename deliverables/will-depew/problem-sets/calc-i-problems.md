# Calculus I — Problem Set
**USNA Validation Prep** | Easy → Hard

---

## Section 1: Limits & Continuity (Easy)

**1.** Evaluate lim(x→3) (x² − 9)/(x − 3).

**2.** Find lim(x→0) sin(5x)/(3x).

**3.** Is f(x) = (x² − 4)/(x − 2) continuous at x = 2? If not, what type of discontinuity?

**4.** Use L'Hôpital's rule: lim(x→∞) (3x² + 1)/(5x² − 2).

**5.** Find all values of x where f(x) = (x + 3)/[(x − 1)(x + 2)] is discontinuous.

---

## Section 2: Derivatives (Easy–Medium)

**6.** Differentiate f(x) = 4x³ − 7x² + 2x − 9.

**7.** Find dy/dx for y = sin(x²)·eˣ. (Product + chain rule)

**8.** Use the quotient rule: f(x) = (3x² − 1)/(x + 4).

**9.** Find f'(x) for f(x) = arctan(2x).

**10.** Use implicit differentiation: x² + y² = 25. Find dy/dx. Then find the slope of the tangent at (3, 4).

---

## Section 3: Related Rates (Medium)

**11.** A spherical balloon is being inflated. The radius increases at 2 cm/s. How fast is the volume increasing when r = 5 cm? (V = 4/3 π r³)

**12.** A ladder 10 m long leans against a wall. The base slides away at 1 m/s. How fast is the top sliding down when the base is 6 m from the wall?

**13.** Water drains from a conical tank (vertex down, height 12 m, radius 4 m) at 2 m³/min. How fast is the water level falling when the depth is 6 m?

---

## Section 4: Optimization (Medium–Hard)

**14.** Find the dimensions of the rectangle with the largest area inscribed in a circle of radius 5.

**15.** A farmer has 300 m of fencing to enclose a rectangular field against a river (no fence needed on river side). What dimensions maximize area?

**16.** Find the maximum value of f(x) = x³ − 3x on the interval [−2, 2].

**17.** A box (no top) is to be made from a 12×12 cm sheet of cardboard by cutting equal squares from each corner. What size squares maximize volume?

---

## Section 5: Integration & FTC (Hard)

**18.** Evaluate ∫(3x² − 4x + 1) dx.

**19.** Compute ∫₀³ (x² − 2x + 1) dx using the FTC.

**20.** Find ∫ x·sin(x²) dx using substitution.

**21.** If G(x) = ∫₁ˣ √(t³ + 1) dt, find G'(x) and G'(2).

**22.** Find the area between y = x² and y = 2x from x = 0 to x = 2.

---

## Challenge Problems

**23.** (Related rates + trig) A street light is mounted 8 m above the ground. A person 1.8 m tall walks away from the base at 1.2 m/s. At what rate is the length of the person's shadow increasing?

**24.** (Optimization) Show that among all rectangles with a fixed perimeter P, the square has the largest area. Prove rigorously using calculus.

**25.** (Curve sketching) For f(x) = x⁴ − 4x³: find critical points, inflection points, intervals of increase/decrease and concavity, then sketch the curve. Identify all local and absolute extrema on [−1, 4].
