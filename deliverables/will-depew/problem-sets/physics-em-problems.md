# Physics — E&M Problem Set (SP212-track)

## Section 1: Electric Force & Field (Easy)

**1.** Two charges: q₁ = +4 μC at x = 0, q₂ = −6 μC at x = 0.3 m. (k = 9×10⁹ N·m²/C²)
(a) Find the magnitude of the force between them.
(b) What is the direction of the force on q₁?

**2.** Find the electric field at x = 0.2 m due to a +5 μC charge at the origin.

**3.** An electron (q = −1.6×10⁻¹⁹ C, m = 9.11×10⁻³¹ kg) enters a uniform field E = 1000 N/C pointing right. The electron moves initially to the right.
(a) Find the force on the electron (direction?).
(b) Find the acceleration.

**4.** Three charges: +3 μC at (0,0), −3 μC at (0.3, 0), +3 μC at (0.6, 0). Find the net force on the −3 μC charge.

## Section 2: Potential & Capacitance (Easy–Medium)

**5.** Find the electric potential at a distance 0.4 m from a +8 μC charge.

**6.** Two parallel plates with area A = 0.02 m² separated by d = 0.001 m. (ε₀ = 8.85×10⁻¹² C²/N·m²)
(a) Find the capacitance.
(b) Find the charge stored when connected to a 12 V battery.
(c) Find the energy stored.

**7.** Three capacitors (C₁ = 2 μF, C₂ = 4 μF, C₃ = 6 μF) are connected in series across 12 V.
(a) Find the equivalent capacitance.
(b) Find the charge on each capacitor.
(c) Find the voltage across each.

## Section 3: DC Circuits (Medium)

**8.** A circuit has a 9 V battery and three resistors: R₁ = 3 Ω in series with a parallel combination of R₂ = 6 Ω and R₃ = 12 Ω.
(a) Find the equivalent resistance.
(b) Find the total current from the battery.
(c) Find the current through R₂ and R₃.

**9.** A 10 Ω resistor and a 100 μF capacitor are connected in series with a 20 V battery. What is the time constant? How long until the capacitor is 63% charged?

**10.** Using Kirchhoff's laws, find the current through each branch:
- Loop 1: 12 V battery, R₁ = 4 Ω, R₃ = 2 Ω (I₁ flows right in top branch)
- Loop 2: 6 V battery, R₂ = 3 Ω, R₃ = 2 Ω (I₂ flows right in bottom branch)
- R₃ is shared (I₃ = I₁ − I₂ or I₁ + I₂ depending on orientation)

(Set up the KVL equations and solve.)

## Section 4: Magnetism & Induction (Hard)

**11.** A proton (q = 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ C, m = 1.67×10⁻²⁷ kg) moves at 3×10⁶ m/s perpendicular to a magnetic field B = 0.2 T.
(a) Find the magnetic force on the proton.
(b) Find the radius of the circular orbit.

**12.** A wire of length L = 0.5 m carries current I = 3 A. It is placed perpendicular to a magnetic field B = 0.4 T. Find the force on the wire.

**13.** A circular loop of radius 0.1 m is in a uniform magnetic field B = 2 T perpendicular to the loop. The field decreases to 0 in 0.5 s.
(a) Find the initial magnetic flux.
(b) Find the induced EMF.
(c) If the loop resistance is 4 Ω, find the induced current.

**14.** A rectangular loop (0.2 m × 0.3 m) moves at 0.5 m/s into a region of uniform B = 0.8 T (perpendicular to loop) as one side of length 0.2 m enters the field.
(a) Find the motional EMF.
(b) If the loop resistance is 2 Ω, find the induced current.
(c) What is the direction of the induced current (Lenz's law)?

## Challenge

**15.** (Gauss's law) A solid insulating sphere of radius R = 0.05 m carries uniform charge density ρ = 10⁻⁶ C/m³.
(a) Find the total charge Q.
(b) Find E at r = 0.03 m (inside).
(c) Find E at r = 0.1 m (outside).

**16.** (RC + energy) A capacitor (C = 50 μF) charged to 100 V is discharged through R = 1 kΩ.
(a) Find the initial stored energy.
(b) Find the time constant.
(c) How much energy remains at t = τ?
(d) How much total energy is dissipated in the resistor? Where did it go?
