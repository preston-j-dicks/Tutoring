# Physics — Mechanics Problem Set (SP211)
**USNA Validation Prep** | Calculus-based, graded Easy → Hard

---

## Section 1: Kinematics (Easy)

**1.** A car starts from rest and accelerates uniformly at 3 m/s². How far does it travel in 6 seconds? What is its speed at t = 6 s?

**2.** A ball is thrown vertically upward at 20 m/s from a height of 10 m. (g = 10 m/s²)
(a) Find the maximum height above the ground.
(b) Find the time to reach the maximum height.
(c) Find the time when the ball hits the ground.

**3.** A projectile is launched at 30° above horizontal with speed 40 m/s (g = 10 m/s²).
(a) Find the maximum height.
(b) Find the time of flight.
(c) Find the range.

**4.** A particle moves along the x-axis with velocity v(t) = 3t² − 6t m/s.
(a) Find the acceleration a(t).
(b) Find the displacement from t = 0 to t = 3 s (integrate).
(c) Find the total distance traveled from t = 0 to t = 3 s.

---

## Section 2: Newton's Laws (Easy–Medium)

**5.** A 5 kg block rests on a frictionless horizontal surface. A horizontal force of 20 N pushes it.
(a) Find the acceleration.
(b) How far does it travel in 3 s starting from rest?

**6.** A 10 kg block on a 30° frictionless incline is released from rest.
(a) Draw a free-body diagram.
(b) Find the acceleration down the incline.
(c) Find the speed after sliding 4 m.

**7.** A 4 kg block is on a surface with μ_k = 0.3. A horizontal force of 25 N is applied. (g = 10 m/s²)
(a) Find the kinetic friction force.
(b) Find the net force and acceleration.

**8.** Two masses m₁ = 3 kg and m₂ = 5 kg are connected by a massless string over a frictionless pulley (Atwood machine). Find the acceleration and the tension in the string.

---

## Section 3: Work & Energy (Medium)

**9.** A 2 kg block is pushed 3 m along a horizontal surface against a friction force of 4 N. The pushing force is 15 N horizontal.
(a) Find the work done by the pushing force.
(b) Find the work done by friction.
(c) Find the net work and the final speed (starting from rest).

**10.** A spring (k = 200 N/m) is compressed 0.15 m. A 0.5 kg mass is placed at the end.
(a) Find the elastic potential energy stored.
(b) Find the speed of the mass when the spring returns to natural length (frictionless surface).

**11.** A 60 kg person runs up a 5 m high staircase in 4 seconds. (g = 10 m/s²)
(a) Find the change in gravitational PE.
(b) Find the minimum power required.

**12.** A 3 kg mass is released from rest at height h = 4 m above the ground. At the bottom, it has speed 7 m/s. How much energy was lost to friction?

---

## Section 4: Momentum & Collisions (Medium)

**13.** A 0.1 kg ball strikes a wall at 10 m/s and bounces back at 8 m/s. If the collision lasts 0.01 s:
(a) Find the change in momentum.
(b) Find the average force exerted by the wall on the ball.

**14.** Two carts on a frictionless track. Cart A (2 kg) moving right at 4 m/s collides with Cart B (3 kg) at rest. They stick together. Find the final velocity.

**15.** A 1 kg ball moving at 6 m/s collides elastically with a 2 kg ball at rest.
(a) Find the final velocities.
(b) Verify kinetic energy is conserved.

---

## Section 5: Rotation & SHM (Hard)

**16.** A solid disk (M = 4 kg, R = 0.3 m) starts from rest and accelerates to 120 rpm in 5 seconds.
(a) Find the angular acceleration α.
(b) Find the number of revolutions made.
(c) Find the final kinetic energy.

**17.** A uniform solid sphere (M = 2 kg, R = 0.1 m) rolls without slipping down a 30° incline of length 2 m, starting from rest.
(a) Use energy conservation to find the speed at the bottom.
(b) Find the angular velocity at the bottom.

**18.** A mass of 0.4 kg is attached to a spring (k = 90 N/m) on a frictionless surface.
(a) Find the angular frequency ω, period T, and frequency f.
(b) If released from x = 0.2 m with zero velocity, write x(t).
(c) Find the maximum speed and the speed at x = 0.1 m.

**19.** A simple pendulum of length 1.5 m is released from an angle of 8°. (g = 10 m/s²)
(a) Find the period.
(b) Find the angular frequency.
(c) Find the maximum speed of the bob (use energy conservation for a pendulum: v_max = √(2gL(1−cos θ₀))).

## Challenge

**20.** (Rotational + translational) A uniform rod of mass M and length L is pivoted at one end. It is released from horizontal. Find its angular velocity when it is vertical (hanging straight down). Use energy conservation.

**21.** (Orbital mechanics) The Moon orbits Earth at radius r = 3.84×10⁸ m with period T = 27.3 days.
(a) Find the orbital speed of the Moon.
(b) Find the centripetal acceleration of the Moon.
(c) Use this to estimate the mass of Earth (G = 6.674×10⁻¹¹).
